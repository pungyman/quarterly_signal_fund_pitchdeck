Strategy posture

This is not a race to parse filings in minutes. It is a bet that cross-sectional fundamentals still help rank the next quarter’s winners.

The model runs after the result window, when complete data exists and entry prices already include the immediate market reaction.

Why the target still makes sense

  • The label excludes the immediate result-day reaction.
  • The model learns a relative ranking over the next quarter.
  • The hypothesis is residual structural signal, not private information.

Six targets, one ranking idea

TargetMetricCutoff

5sharpe_highTop 20%

High5

Captures intraday spikes. Natural for take-profit limit orders, but more optimistic and noisier.

Close5

Captures sustained buying pressure. More conservative and better aligned with EOD realism.

Sharpe variants

Reward upside while penalizing volatility, shifting the objective toward risk-adjusted winners.

Tuning11 / 18
The combinatorial problem
9.8M

Manual combinations from only five choices across ten parameters. Real strategies quickly exceed that.

Selection levers

Top-k, category counts, weighting schemes, probability thresholds, sorting choices.

Exit levers

Flat, tiered, ATR, pivot, independent TP/SL modes and mode-specific parameters.

Market levers

Index moving averages, regime thresholds, volatility adjustments, risk-free cash behavior.
